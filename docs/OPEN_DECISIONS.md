| OD-19 | Who may grant/withdraw staff duties ("desks"), and whether separation of duties is mandatory | ICT + Management | Duty assignments (`duty_user`); payslip release control (PAY-09) | **Decided (20 Oct 2026):** (a) ICT and Institutional superadmins (`account.manage`) may assign duties for any account; a **unit lead** (`duty.lead`) may also assign/withdraw duties for colleagues in their **own department and unit** only, never for their own account, and never account-wide administrative duties (`account.manage`, `audit.view`, `duty.lead`). Each unit's administrative role (Registry, Bursary, ICT) and HOD carry the `duty.lead` baseline. (b) **Separation of duties is mandatory:** `bursary.payslip_release` must be held by a **different** officer than `bursary.payslip_upload`; the uploader may never release their own batch, and the release duty cannot be granted to an account already holding the upload duty. | decided |
| OD-20 | Registry internal authority chain: Head of HR review, Registrar final Registry approval, Director escalation, and delegation of Registry duties | Registry + Management | Registry profile-change review, duty assignments, correspondence workflows, Registry dashboards | **Decided (22 Oct 2026):** (a) routine Registry work rises **officer → Head of HR (`registry.head_review`) → Registrar (`registry.registrar_approve`)**; the Registrar heads the Registry department and gives final Registry approval. The **Director (`registry.director_escalation`)** acts only where a configured workflow routes a matter to the Director — the application does not infer which institutional matters are reserved. (b) The Head of HR and the Registrar may assign ordinary Registry desk duties to Registry officers (`DutyCatalog::DELEGATED_LEAD_DUTIES`), but the three chain duties are **explicit grants only**: they are not role baselines, cannot be self-granted, and never include account-wide administrative duties. (c) Seeded correspondence workflows are **pilot defaults**, fully configurable and versioned; editing a definition publishes a new version and never rewrites a document already in progress. Changing routing does not itself decide any reserved-matter policy. | decided |
